# Weekly Joint Strength Plan (for maintenance & joint resilience)

**Do the strength routine after a light warm-up (5 min elliptical or leg swings).**

---

## Monday – Cardio + Isometrics
| Type | Session |
|------|---------|
| Cardio | Elliptical 30 min, easy pace (Zone 2: 132–151 bpm) |
| Strength | Wall sit 3 × 40 sec<br>Plank 3 × 40 sec<br>Single-leg balance (slight knee bend) 3 × 30 sec per leg |

---

## Tuesday – Glutes & Hips (Root of most joint issues)
| Type | Session |
|------|---------|
| Strength | Clamshells 3 × 15 per side<br>Side-lying leg raises 3 × 15 per side<br>Single-leg glute bridge 3 × 12 per leg<br>Lateral leg raises (standing, no band) 3 × 12 per leg |

---

## Wednesday – Cardio Only (active recovery)
| Type | Session |
|------|---------|
| Cardio | Elliptical 25–30 min, very easy (Zone 1–2: 113–151 bpm) |
| Mobility | Leg swings (forward/back + side/side) 15 each<br>Deep squat hold (supported if needed) 3 × 20 sec |

---

## Thursday – Eccentrics & Tendon Strength
| Type | Session |
|------|---------|
| Strength | Eccentric heel raises (slow down, 3 sec) 3 × 12<br>Eccentric split squat (lower slow, step back up) 3 × 8 per leg<br>Single-leg Romanian deadlift (no weight, slow control) 3 × 10 per leg |

---

## Friday – Cardio + Core
| Type | Session |
|------|---------|
| Cardio | Elliptical 30 min, moderate pace (Zone 2: 132–151 bpm) |
| Strength | Side plank 3 × 30 sec per side<br>Dead bug (slow, controlled) 3 × 10 per side<br>Glute bridge (both legs) 3 × 15 |

---

## Saturday – Full Joint Routine (Short but effective)
| Type | Session |
|------|---------|
| Strength | Wall sit 2 × 45 sec<br>Clamshells 2 × 20 per side<br>Single-leg balance 2 × 40 sec per leg<br>Eccentric heel raises 2 × 15 |

---

## Sunday – Rest
| Type | Session |
|------|---------|
| Rest | Complete rest or light stretching (hamstrings, quads, glutes, hip flexors) |

## Notes
- Warm up 1–2 km easy (Zone 1–2: 113–151 bpm) before intervals and tempo runs.
- Cool down 1–2 km easy (Zone 1–2) after all workouts.
- For easy and long runs, keep heart rate strictly in **Zone 2 (132–151 bpm)** — if it drifts higher, slow down.
- Tempo runs should feel "comfortably hard" — you can speak in short sentences but not hold a conversation.
- If a session feels too hard, prioritize heart rate targets over pace targets.
- Stay hydrated, prioritize sleep, and do light mobility work on rest days.
- Test a **5K time trial** around Week 6 to check progress (target: 21:30–22:00).
