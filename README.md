# 10K Training Plan: 52 min → 45 min (12 Weeks)
**Pace targets:**  
- Easy: 5:30–6:00/km  
- Tempo (starting): 4:50–5:00/km  
- Tempo (target by end): 4:35–4:45/km  
- Interval pace: 4:15–4:25/km  
- Goal 10K pace: 4:30/km  

**Heart Rate Zones (31M):**  
- Easy / Long Run: **Zone 2 (132–151 bpm)**  
- Tempo: **Zone 3 (151–164 bpm)**  
- Intervals: **Zone 4–5 (164–189 bpm)**  
- Warm-up / Cool-down: **Zone 1–2 (113–151 bpm)**

## Notes
- Warm up 1–2 km easy (Zone 1–2: 113–151 bpm) before intervals and tempo runs.
- Cool down 1–2 km easy (Zone 1–2) after all workouts.
- For easy and long runs, keep heart rate strictly in **Zone 2 (132–151 bpm)** — if it drifts higher, slow down.
- Tempo runs should feel "comfortably hard" — you can speak in short sentences but not hold a conversation.
- If a session feels too hard, prioritize heart rate targets over pace targets.
- Stay hydrated, prioritize sleep, and do light mobility work on rest days.
- Test a **5K time trial** around Week 6 to check progress (target: 21:30–22:00).
