# 10K Sub-45:00 Training Plan (12 Weeks)

### Training Zones (based on LT @ 182 bpm)
| Zone | Name | Pace | Heart Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z1 | Easy/Recovery | >6:00/km | <155 bpm |
| Z2 | Aerobic base | 5:45–6:00/km | 155–165 bpm |
| Z3 | Tempo / LT | 4:45–5:05/km | 170–178 bpm |
| Z4 | Sub-LT / 10K pace | 4:25–4:45/km | 178–185 bpm |
| Z5 | VO₂ Max | 4:00–4:20/km | >185 bpm |

---

## Plan Structure

- **Weeks 1–4** — Base & Threshold Introduction (~35–50 km/week)
- **Weeks 5–8** — Threshold Development & Race-Pace Work (~39–56 km/week)
- **Weeks 9–11** — Peak & Sharpening (~42–57 km/week)
- **Week 12** — Taper & Race (~27 km/week)

> **Key**: Quality > quantity. Nail paces on hard days; keep easy days genuinely easy.

## Race Day Strategy

### Pacing Plan (target: 44:45)
| Section | Pace | Cumulative Time |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 km (settle in) | 4:33–4:35/km | ~9:06 |
| 2–7 km (controlled race pace) | 4:28–4:30/km | ~31:30 |
| 7–9 km (push threshold) | 4:25–4:28/km | ~40:25 |
| 9–10 km (empty the tank) | 4:15–4:20/km | **~44:40–44:50** |

> ⚠️ **Do not go out faster than 4:32/km for the first kilometre.** The most common mistake at this distance is burning LT reserves in the first 2 km.

---

## Supplementary Guidelines

### Strength & Injury Prevention (2× per week)
- **Hip strengthening**: clamshells, single-leg glute bridges, lateral band walks (3 × 15 each)
- **Calf/Achilles**: single-leg calf raises, 3 × 20 each leg
- **Core**: dead bugs, Copenhagen planks, bird-dogs
- **Mobility**: daily hip flexor and hamstring stretching (5–10 min)
